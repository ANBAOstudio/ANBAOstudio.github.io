Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ANBAO Studio handles information in our mobile games and related services.

Information we may collect

Our games may use third-party services that collect device information, app activity, approximate location derived from IP address, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, crash data, and interactions with advertisements.

Advertising

We may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may process information to provide, measure, personalize, and prevent fraud in advertising, subject to applicable consent requirements and user settings.

Analytics and crash reporting

We may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand game usage, improve performance, and diagnose crashes or technical issues.

Children

Our services are not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Where required, we configure advertising and data processing in accordance with applicable child-directed treatment and age-related requirements.

Data retention and security

Information processed by third-party providers is retained according to their policies. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may control certain permissions and advertising preferences through your device settings. Depending on your region, you may also have rights to access, delete, or restrict certain data.

Third-party services

Our games may use services provided by Google, including Google Play Services, AdMob, Firebase Analytics, and Firebase Crashlytics. Their own privacy policies govern their processing activities.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our games, services, or legal obligations change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to anbaostudiogame@gmail.com.
